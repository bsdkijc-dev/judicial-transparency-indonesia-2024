# Applicable Accounting Framework & Reporting Scope — FY 2024

* **Reporting Entity:** Mahkamah Agung Republik Indonesia (Bagian Anggaran `005` / UAPA), covering all **4 judicial jurisdictions** (*Peradilan Umum*, *Peradilan Agama*, *Peradilan Militer dan Tata Usaha Negara*, and *Kepaniteraan*) plus supporting Eselon I units.
* **Primary Standard:** **PP No. 71 Tahun 2010** concerning *Standar Akuntansi Pemerintahan* (SAP) — **Accrual Basis** (*Basis Akrual*) for Neraca, Laporan Operasional (LO), and Laporan Perubahan Ekuitas (LPE), and **Cash/Budgetary Basis** (*Basis Kas*) for Laporan Realisasi Anggaran (LRA).
* **Financial Management Systems Verified in Report:** **SAKTI** (core accounting, budget execution, and BMN asset module), **SPAN** (treasury disbursement/receipts), and **SatuDJA** (revenue target comparison).
* **Preserved Classifications:** Original Indonesian account codes (`51` Belanja Pegawai, `52` Belanja Barang, `53` Belanja Modal, `425xxx` PNBP), program codes (`005.BF` Penegakan dan Pelayanan Hukum, `005.WA` Dukungan Manajemen), and original Bahasa Indonesia statement terminology are preserved alongside English analytical descriptions.
